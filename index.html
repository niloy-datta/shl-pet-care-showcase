Frequently Asked Procurement Questions

Key commercial and operational answers for international procurement managers, distributors, and retail buyers.

Our standard MOQ for custom private label packaging (custom printed polybags/boxes) is typically one 20GP container per product line, or equivalent palletized volume depending on SKU configuration. For standard neutral packaging or initial commercial evaluation runs, smaller trial quantities may be accommodated upon review.
We maintain stock of standard specification samples (diapers and training pads across all sizes). Sample kits are typically prepared and dispatched via international air express (DHL/FedEx) within 24 to 48 hours. Prospective buyers can request specific size combinations through our quotation form.
We provide full turnkey packaging customisation: client artwork application on primary polybags, branded retail dispenser cartons, custom count packs (e.g. 10, 20, 40, 50, 100 count), localized multi-language instructions, EAN/UPC barcoding, and export master carton marking.
Initial customized OEM production runs generally take 25 to 35 days following packaging artwork sign-off and deposit confirmation. Repeat orders are typically fulfilled in 20 to 25 days. Ocean freight transit times vary by destination port (e.g., approximately 14–20 days to West Coast USA, 28–35 days to European main ports).
We routinely quote on standard international commercial terms: FOB (Shanghai or Ningbo Port), CIF (named destination port worldwide), and DDP (delivered duty paid to buyer warehouse, subject to local customs feasibility in your jurisdiction). Air freight is available for urgent requirements.
Partner manufacturing facilities follow ISO 9001:2015 protocols and AQL 2.5 acceptance sampling. Every production batch undergoes SAP absorbency speed tests, seal tension checks, and carton drop tests. Formal factory inspection reports and certificates of analysis are supplied with export documentation.
Formal proforma quotations are issued within 24 hours of receiving your target volume, product specifications, and destination port. Standard international commercial payment terms include T/T (wire transfer with production deposit and balance against Bill of Lading) or confirmed Letter of Credit (L/C) for established corporate accounts.
